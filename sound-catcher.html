How to get clean system audio
  1. Pick “System / tab audio”, then press Start.
  2. In the popup, choose a screen (Windows) or a tab (Mac).
  3. Tick “Share system audio” or “Share tab audio”.
  4. Play your sound. Keep the peak below 0 dB.
